# Model council — 2026-11-25 (why the models trail the market)

Participants (Claude sub-agents, independent, then one discussion round and a skeptic review):
ML lead, data scientist, skeptic. Evidence and scripts: session scratchpad `model-council/` (not committed; DB copies).

## Findings
* **Engine A is at a plateau.** 13 hyperparameter variants were tested walk-forward (holdout 2025-08→2026-11,
  n = 4,955, week-clustered bootstrap): time decay, ridge, Elo K, refit cadence, newcomer prior, per-league weights
  and per-league intercepts. All stayed within ±0.001 log loss. **REJECTED: stop tuning.**
* **Most of the gap to the market is information we do not have**: transfers, injuries, lineups, budgets of
  promoted teams. Blending our model with the pre-match price gives our model weight ≈ 0. Our probabilities add
  nothing to the market's.
* **Real gap is larger than reported.** About 5 % of football-data.co.uk BFE (exchange) pre-match prices are
  corrupt (overround 23–196 %), and BFE was the first reference book everywhere. Against a clean reference,
  Engine A is +0.0158 log loss worse than the market (previously reported +0.0126).
* **Where A loses most:**
  * ENG2, FRA1, ESP1 and ITA1 (+0.02 each);
  * close matches;
  * promoted teams (+0.021).

  A slightly *beats* the market on draws.

## Disagreements and rulings (skeptic)
- **Provider down-marking:** a request skipped while TheSportsDB is marked down, or still locked out after a second 429, is **never counted** towards the 6 h circuit breaker. The overdue step stops on "marked down" as well as "429". football-data.org has its own `request()` and is unaffected.
- **Rate limits:**
  - At most **one** lock-out wait per provider per process. A lasting limit (quota) could otherwise reach the 80-min job timeout (30 URLs × 3 × 130 s).
  - Old cached data is served early only when the block is > 30 s.
  - The `rate_limited` label is cleared after a 5xx.
- **`keepOnToday`:** postponed, cancelled and abandoned matches are excluded.

## Disagreements and rulings (skeptic)
- **Log buffering:** agreed. Buffered lines keep their **event time** (`ts=`). Known limit: lines are lost if the process dies inside the scope.
- **429:**
  - The rate state (last request, cool-down) is now **shared by every instance** of a provider. `collect.provider()` builds new instances, so the overdue step could fire straight after the league pass's 429.
  - An HTTP-date `Retry-After` no longer raises.
  - The 5 s spacing is a measured guess (first 429 after 13–14 calls/min); the next cycle checks that 429s disappear.
- **Handover look-back:** 10 minutes, not 3 (queue and start-up time). If GitHub cannot be read, backfilled minutes are **skipped**: a duplicate pending run could cancel a queued critical one. The watchdog's catch-up is the backstop.

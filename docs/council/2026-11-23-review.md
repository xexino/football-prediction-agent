## 1. Executive summary

**Where we are.** Football Intelligence Lab is an honest, well-built **probability laboratory with no track record yet**.
* The engineering foundations are real:
  * Trigger-enforced immutable predictions.
  * Fail-closed restore.
  * 110 passing tests.
  * Walk-forward validation that the council reproduced **exactly**.
* The record is empty. Production has 121 Engine A predictions (all NO BET), 130 Engine B predictions, **0 virtual bets, 0 settlements and 0 Engine B validation rows**.
* Autonomy is not yet proven. The first-ever *scheduled* Actions run started at 01:57 UTC today, about 77 minutes after its slot.

**What the evidence says about prediction quality.**
* **1X2 (Engine A):** log loss 0.9949, which beats naive (1.0768) but is **worse than the de-vigged market (0.9819)**.
* **1X2 (Engine B):** **significantly worse than Engine A** (ΔLL +0.0051, 95% CI [+0.0005, +0.0096]).
* **Over/under 2.5 goals:** Engine B beats Engine A, but is significantly worse than the market.
* **Corners:** no skill over the league-mean baseline.
* **Betting backtest (Engine A):** 65 bets, ROI −22.0%, 95% CI −50%…+8.5%. **No edge has been demonstrated.**

**The council's verdict on direction.** Stop presenting this as a value-betting product. Become the **most verifiable football forecast lab on the internet**:
* timestamped, immutable, pre-registered predictions;
* scored publicly against the market and naive baselines, with sample sizes and confidence intervals;
* honest match analysis built only from real data.

Live football should stay **delayed and labelled** until a minute-level feed is legitimately available. Do not compete with SofaScore, FotMob or Flashscore on live data.

**What to do first** (all small, all before the first bet settles):
1. Never publish an empty dashboard after a failed restore (QA-3).
2. Make the integrity check unable to "heal" what it checks, and freeze settled-bet fields (QA-2 + QA-1).
3. Make failures audible: GitHub issue on failure or after 8 h without a successful run, and the owner re-saves the cron (OP-1/OP-4).
4. Fix three misleading displays: mobile disclaimer, EV shown on flagged odds, Champions League predictions from an isolated rating pool (UX-1/UX-5/DS-1 label).
5. Freeze the evaluation protocol and start an append-only public prediction ledger *now*, while n = 0 (RK-1 + RK-9 + GR-1).

## 4. Agent-by-agent findings

Each specialist wrote an independent report (sections 1–7: situation, evidence, strengths, weaknesses, risks, recommendations with status, priority, effort, dependencies and acceptance criteria, and a top 3). They then cross-reviewed at least two peers. Only the headline findings are summarised here; the files hold the evidence.

### Agent 1 — Chief Product Officer ([report](https://github.com/xexino/football-prediction-agent/blob/main/docs/council/2026-11-23/agent01_product.md))
* **Positioning.** The defensible value is an *auditable probability lab*, not tips. Users can't be sold a "value bet" product whose own backtest shows ROI −22%.
* **Front door.** Make a **Track Record** page the homepage (PM-2).
* **Daily return.** A "what changed today" briefing (PM-5) and following favourite teams (PM-6, localStorage, no accounts).
* **Trust papercuts** (PM-8):
  * The coverage matrix says football-data.org is "not configured" although the key works.
  * The README described the API-Football adapter wrongly.
  * Model goal expectancy is labelled "xG".
* **Targets:**
  * scheduled-run success ≥ 95% over 30 days;
  * ≥ 1,500 evaluated live predictions by month 3;
  * live 1X2 log loss within 0.015 of the market;
  * live ECE ≤ 0.03;
  * fixtures with an odds reference: from 15% (21/141, corrected in cross-review) to ≥ 70%.
* **Out of scope for now:** player props, LLM layer, persistent live poller.

### Agent 2 — Senior Football Data Scientist ([report](https://github.com/xexino/football-prediction-agent/blob/main/docs/council/2026-11-23/agent02_data.md))
* **DS-1.** 135 UEFA-competition teams exist as disconnected `country='Europe'` duplicates; at least 45 have domestic twins. Champions League predictions therefore come from an **isolated rating pool**.
* **DS-2.** The Andorra national team is merged with FC Andorra (`teamnames.py:62`, which matches when the country is empty). The alias "athletic" maps to Atlético. Renames split teams. There are 7 duplicate fixtures and 15 same-day doubles.
* **Markets with odds:** only 1X2 and O/U 2.5 have odds; BTTS, corners and cards have none. There are 0 player rows.
* **Usable features nobody uses:**
  * per-league home advantage (0.17–0.45 goals);
  * a promotion effect;
  * shots and corners;
  * half-time scores (26,698);
  * xG for 2,033 matches.
  * Referee names exist for ENG/SCO only.
* **Self-correction:** Engine A already re-seeds promoted teams; only Engine B starts them at Elo 1500.

### Agent 3 — ML / Quantitative Modelling ([report](https://github.com/xexino/football-prediction-agent/blob/main/docs/council/2026-11-23/agent03_ml.md))
* **Validation reproduced exactly.** Log loss: A 0.9949, B 0.9999, market 0.9819.
* **1X2:** B is significantly worse than A (paired bootstrap CI excludes 0).
* **O/U 2.5:** B beats A but is significantly worse than the market.
* **Documented numbers are not in production.** `backtest_runs` holds the older format, `eb_validation_runs` is empty, and the 470-bet Engine B 1X2 result cannot be regenerated, because 1X2 betting is now disabled in code.
* **Tested and rejected:** calibration, A+B averaging and market stacking gave **no significant gain** (e.g. A+B ΔLL −0.0008, CI includes 0).
* **Coverage gap:** 2.7% of matches are unscored (promoted teams). The live in-play model is unvalidated.
* **Order of work:** reproducible validation in production → CLV definition → tuning on a pre-2025 period with a ≥ 1,500-match forward holdout → promotion priors → shrink fitting.
* **Gradient boosting: NOT RECOMMENDED** (no feature advantage, more overfitting surface).

### Agent 4 — Match Analysis Specialist ([report](https://github.com/xexino/football-prediction-agent/blob/main/docs/council/2026-11-23/agent04_analysis.md))
* **Over-confident wording.** 102 of 130 reports say "high confidence". A 47% favourite is called a "clear favourite". The "main uncertainty" given is often an effect of under 10%.
* **The analysis compares only half the matchup** (`insights.py:56-60`), and the draw-favourite case is phrased wrongly.
* **Corners reliability figures are hard-coded** (`insights.py:43`).
* **Boilerplate.** The most likely score is 1-1 in 86% of reports.
* **Lessons from public products** (FotMob, SofaScore, Flashscore; public features only, no internals claimed): users value form strips, head-to-head and a clear probability bar.
* **Recommendations:**
  * calibrated labels from absolute probabilities (MA-1);
  * all four attack/defence pairings plus a cross-league flag (MA-2/6);
  * later: form, strengths and xG panels built from data already stored (MA-4/5/8).
* Momentum and player insights are **BLOCKED** (no event or player data).

### Agent 5 — Data Provider & API Architect ([report](https://github.com/xexino/football-prediction-agent/blob/main/docs/council/2026-11-23/agent05_providers.md))
* **What exists for API-Football:** only the *live* adapter. It has no fixtures, lineups or injuries endpoints, yet it declares `capabilities["lineups"]=True`, which is wrong.
* **Quota maths** (DP-1):
  * The Odds API: about 290 credits/day against a 500/month free plan.
  * API-Football: 48–192 requests/day against 100/day.
  * **A persisted quota guard must exist before any key is added.**
* **DP-3: an error-masking bug** (`collect.py:121-125`): `mark_success` runs after `mark_error`, so the Odds API circuit breaker can never open (strengthened by Agent 9).
* **DP-4: live freshness is wrong.** `provider_updated_at=now` (`live.py:175`) measures freshness from retrieval time, not from the provider's update time.
* **Questionable mappings:** some `APIF_IDS` (e.g. WCQ=1, UNL=5) need verification.
* **DP-10, licensing:** about 348k football-data.co.uk odds rows are republished in the public DB release. Redistribution terms must be recorded.
* **Paid options** (owner approval needed):

### Agent 6 — Live Systems Engineer ([report](https://github.com/xexino/football-prediction-agent/blob/main/docs/council/2026-11-23/agent06_live.md))
* **0 live snapshots so far.** The live layer has never seen an in-play match in production.
* **LV-1:** the browser never recomputes labels. Pages caches for 600 s and the poll runs every 20 min, so a "LIVE" badge can be 15–40 minutes old.
* **LV-2:** finished matches stay "live" for up to 3 h.
* **LV-3:** every live run downloads and re-uploads the 37.7 MB DB (about 1.5 GB/day, ~130 GB of artifacts).
* **LV-4:** one bad match rolls back the whole batch.
* **LV-5:** VAR reversals and second yellows are not handled.
* **LV-7:** the 11–23 UTC poll window misses Americas and Asia evenings.
* **Staged plan:** delayed → near-live → real-time. **Never call GitHub Pages "live".**

### Agent 7 — Quant Risk & Virtual Betting ([report](https://github.com/xexino/football-prediction-agent/blob/main/docs/council/2026-11-23/agent07_risk.md))
* **Engine A backtest in prod:** 65 bets, ROI **−22.0%**, bootstrap CI −50%…+8.5%. Backtests bypass the live gates and the rules were tuned in-sample.
* **CLV is weak.**
  * It uses a same-bookmaker ratio and is never back-filled.
  * Random selection already shows +0.1–0.4% CLV.
  * Engine B 1X2 showing +1.4% CLV alongside −23.5% ROI is a possible bug.
* **Reference odds are fragile.**
  * Pinnacle (PS) odds end in January 2026.
  * The Betfair-exchange reference (BFE) contains corrupt prices (e.g. a 1.10 draw against 3.75 elsewhere).
  * Odds age is measured from download time.
* **Power analysis:** proving ROI needs 2,800–15,700 bets, while CLV needs only about 125–350 bets. **CLV is the realistic evidence metric.**
* **Recommendations:**
  * RK-1: pre-register the rules (hash).
  * RK-2: CLV against the de-vigged consensus close, minus random drift.
  * RK-3: backtests replay the live gates.
  * RK-6: a "no edge demonstrated" banner with n and CI.
* **Kelly staking: NOT RECOMMENDED.**

## 6. Critical risks

1. **Invisible outage.**
   * There is no alert path; failure mail goes to a noreply cron author.
   * A failed restore publishes a fresh-looking **empty** dashboard (QA-3).
   * Together these could hide a stoppage indefinitely.
2. **A permanent gap in the track record.** A prediction must exist before kickoff. Missed or cancelled 3-hourly runs, whether from a live run cancelling them or a delayed schedule, leave holes that can never be filled.
3. **A ledger that cannot be verified.**
   * Settled payout, profit and CLV are mutable (QA-1).
   * The integrity check self-heals (QA-2).
   * The public site and DB are overwritten each run, so outsiders can't verify anything (GR-1).
4. **Quota and cost if a key is added too early.** The Odds API (about 290 credits/day against 500/month) and API-Football (up to about 156–192 requests/day against 100/day) would exhaust free plans before a quota guard exists (DP-1).
5. **Misleading presentation.**
   * The mobile layout hides the simulation disclaimer.
   * The analyst says "high confidence" on 78% of reports.
   * "LIVE" badges could be 15–40 min old once a minute feed is added.
   * UEFA-cup predictions come from an isolated rating pool.
6. **Data licensing (UNKNOWN).** About 348k third-party odds rows are republished in a public release asset. The terms have not been recorded (DP-10).
7. **Statistical over-reading.** 65 backtest bets cannot show an edge either way: proving ROI needs ≥ 2,800 bets. Documentation previously showed ROI −25% (scratch copy) while production shows −22.0%.

## 7. Recommended product direction: answers to the 14 strategy questions

1. **What is Football Intelligence Lab today?** An autonomous, open-source football *probability* lab. Two engines, 37+ leagues, a virtual-betting simulator that correctly says NO BET, and a rich static dashboard. It has no live track record yet.
2. **Actual technical maturity?** Beta.
   * Engineering hygiene is high: immutability triggers, fail-closed restore, 110 tests, reproducible validation.
   * Operations are unproven (first scheduled run today, no alerting).
   * Statistical evidence is limited to backtests.
3. **Strongest capabilities?**
   * Honest validation that reproduces exactly.
   * Immutable, timestamped predictions with stored inputs.
   * Broad free history (58k fixtures, about 348k odds rows).
   * A disciplined NO BET default.
   * Evidence-only match reports.
   * Zero running cost.
4. **Biggest weaknesses?**
   * No track record.
   * Unproven autonomy without alerts.
   * Three integrity gaps (QA-1/2/3).
   * Team-identity errors.
   * Over-confident analyst wording.
   * A cluttered, conflicting dashboard on mobile.
5. **What holds back prediction quality?**
   * Data identity: duplicate UEFA teams and the Andorra merge.
   * Promoted-team cold starts in Engine B.
   * In-sample-tuned rules.
   * No production validation record.
   * A reference market with gaps (PS ended in January 2026; BFE glitches).
   * Not model sophistication: calibration, stacking and GBM showed no gain.
6. **What holds back global coverage?**
   * No free legitimate source for Africa/CAF, lineups or injuries.
   * The API-Football pre-match adapter does not exist.
   * Free plans have small quotas, and no quota guard exists.
   * Odds exist for only about 15% of next-7-day fixtures.
7. **What holds back live football?**
   * No minute-level feed (football-data.org free is delayed score/status only).
   * GitHub Actions cron is not real time (77 min late today).
   * Static Pages caching.
   * Each live run moves the full DB.
   * Label freshness is measured from retrieval time.
8. **What holds back the dashboard?**
   * Four inconsistent match views.
   * Engine A, Engine B and market numbers unlabelled.
   * Mobile navigation and overflow.
   * Heavy payloads.
   * No Track Record front door.
   * Error-flagged odds still shown.
9. **What should we build next?** In order:
   1. Integrity and alerting fixes.
   2. The pre-registered protocol plus the append-only ledger.
   3. Truthful UI and analyst labels.
   4. A Track Record page with CLV against the consensus close.
   5. Team-identity fixes.
   6. Then, and only then, tuning experiments with a forward holdout.
10. **What should we stop doing?**
    * Running about 39 ungated live jobs a day.
    * Presenting backtest ROI without CI and n.
    * Calling model goal expectancy "xG".
    * Saying "high confidence" without validation.
    * Planning paid feeds or LLM layers before the basics are verified.
11. **What should we simplify?**
    * Navigation: 12 → 4 task-based sections.
    * One canonical match page.
    * One provider-status truth.
    * One live persistence path (gate it, don't add `live.db` or Cloudflare).
    * One benchmark set: market close, naive and Elo-only, all already stored.
12. **What should we measure?**
    * **Operations:** scheduled-run success ≥ 95% over 30 days, schedule delay, 0 integrity failures, dashboard age < 4 h.
    * **Evidence:** live log loss against the market close (target: within 0.015), ECE ≤ 0.03, CLV against the de-vigged consensus close minus random-selection drift (power: about 125–350 bets), predictions published before kickoff (target 100% of eligible fixtures).
    * **Product:** fixtures with an odds reference (15% → 70%); returning visitors (UNKNOWN, no analytics yet).
13. **What would make it genuinely competitive?** Nobody in the consumer football space publishes immutable, pre-registered, externally timestamped forecasts scored openly against the market with CIs (council observation based on public features only). If we do that continuously for one or two seasons, the lab becomes a citable public benchmark.
14. **Most valuable direction for the next 90 days.** "**Prove it.**" Make the pipeline trustworthy and audible, freeze the protocol, and publish a growing, verifiable track record. Accept that the honest headline may be "close to the market, no betting edge".
